Privacy Policy

Cubyard Drive Backup · Last updated November 25, 2026

Who we are

Cubyard Drive Backup is the app that self-hosted Cubyard panels use to store their encrypted backups in the panel operator's own Google Drive or OneDrive (see the home page). Each panel is installed and run by its operator on the operator's own server. The Cubyard project publishes the software and this app; it does not run any service that receives, stores or processes your data.

What the app accesses

  • Google Drive: the drive.file scope only. The panel can create, read and delete only the files and folders it created itself. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.
  • OneDrive: Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder only. The panel can use only its own folder under Apps in your OneDrive. It also requests offline_access to keep backing up without asking you to sign in each time.
  • Your account name and email address (Google's basic profile through the Drive API, Microsoft's User.Read), used only to show in the panel which account is connected.

How the data is used

  • To upload the panel's backup files to the backup folder, to read them back when checking the connection or restoring the panel, and to delete old backups the panel uploaded, according to the operator's retention settings.
  • Backup files are encrypted on the operator's server before upload; the drive provider cannot read their contents.
  • The data is not used for advertising, is not sold or shared with anyone, and is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where the data is kept

The authorization token is stored, encrypted, in the database of the operator's own panel, and is sent only to Google or Microsoft. Backups are stored only in the operator's drive. Nothing is sent to the Cubyard project or any third party.

The token is kept until the operator removes the storage from the panel or revokes access. Backups stay in the drive until the panel deletes them under the retention settings or the operator deletes them.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time: for Google, at myaccount.google.com/permissions; for Microsoft, at account.live.com/consent/Manage. Removing the storage in the panel deletes the stored token. Backups already in your drive remain until you delete them.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date. Questions can be raised at github.com/Connor3773/cubyard-app/issues.

隐私权政策

Cubyard Drive Backup · 最后更新：2026 年 11 月 25 日

我们是谁

Cubyard Drive Backup 是经营者自行部署的 Cubyard 面板用来把已加密的备份存放到经营者自己的 Google Drive 或 OneDrive 的应用（见首页）。每个面板由经营者安装在自己的服务器上并自行运行。Cubyard 项目发布软件和本应用，但不运行任何接收、存储或处理您的数据的服务。

应用访问的内容

  • Google Drive：仅 drive.file 范围。面板只能创建、读取和删除它自己建的文件和文件夹，看不到也无法改动网盘中的其他文件。
  • OneDrive：仅 Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder。面板只能使用 OneDrive“应用”下属于它自己的文件夹；另申请 offline_access，以便持续备份而无需每次登录。
  • 账号名称和邮箱（通过 Drive API 读取的谷歌基本资料、微软的 User.Read），仅用于在面板中显示连接的是哪个账号。

数据的用途

  • 把面板的备份文件上传到备份文件夹；在测试连接或恢复面板时读回；按经营者的保留设置删除面板自己上传的旧备份。
  • 备份文件在经营者的服务器上加密后才上传，网盘服务商无法读取其内容。
  • 数据不用于广告，不出售，不与任何人共享，也不用于训练人工智能或机器学习模型。
  • 对从 Google API 获得的信息的使用遵守Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括其中的“有限使用”要求。

数据存放在哪里

授权令牌加密保存在经营者自己的面板的数据库中，只发往谷歌或微软；备份只存放在经营者的网盘中。不向 Cubyard 项目或任何第三方发送数据。

令牌保留到经营者在面板中删除该存储或撤销授权为止；备份保留到面板按保留设置删除，或经营者自行删除为止。

撤销授权

您可以随时撤销授权：谷歌账号在 myaccount.google.com/permissions，微软账号在 account.live.com/consent/Manage。在面板中删除该存储会同时删除保存的令牌；已在网盘中的备份保留到您自行删除为止。

变更和联系

本政策的变更将在本页发布并更新日期。如有问题，请在 github.com/Connor3773/cubyard-app/issues 提出。
